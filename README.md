### 📊 Projects

Each project follows a full analytics workflow — from raw data to business recommendation.

**🏛️ [India GST Revenue Analysis](https://github.com/arunc12/India-GST-Revenue-Analysis)** &nbsp;`Python` `Power BI`
> Analyzed India's GST collections (2017–2026) across 36 states & UTs on real government data. Performed EDA, built forecasts, and delivered interactive dashboards revealing regional revenue trends.

**🛒 [E-Commerce Sales Analytics](https://github.com/arunc12/E-Commerce-Sales-Analytics-Excel-Capstone-)** &nbsp;`Excel`
> Built a star-schema model over 2,000 transactions, applying pivot tables and lookup formulas to surface sales patterns and category performance.

**💊 [Supplement Sales Analysis](https://github.com/arunc12/Mini-Project-Supplement-Sales-Analysis-In-Power-BI)** &nbsp;`Power BI`
> Designed a Power BI dashboard over 4,385 rows of sales data across USA, UK & Canada to track revenue by product and region.

**👥 [HR Employee Attrition](https://github.com/arunc12/hr-analytics-employee-attrition-sql)** &nbsp;`MySQL`
> Ran a 25-query SQL analysis on 423 employees to profile attrition drivers and quantify workforce risk for a consulting firm.

**🎓 [eLearning Purchase Analysis](https://github.com/arunc12/eLearning-Purchase-Analysis-MySQL)** &nbsp;`MySQL`
> Modeled a normalized 3-table schema to analyze an eLearning platform's purchase behavior across course categories.

**⚽ [Premier League Investment](https://github.com/arunc12/Premier-League-Investment-Analysis)** &nbsp;`Python` `Power BI`
> Built a 100-point scoring model to shortlist 40 clubs for investment, combining EDA with an interactive decision dashboard.
